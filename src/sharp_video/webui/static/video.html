      Drop in an ordinary video and it is converted here — SHARP spatializes each frame into 9 views, TMIV encodes them as MIV — and then previewed. A clip from the library, or a .miv you drop in, goes straight to the preview.

      Either way you are watching what TmivDecoder, the MPEG reference decoder, reconstructs, exactly as a player would see it.

      While it plays, move your pointer across the video to look around the scene, or switch to the 3×3 grid to see every camera at once.
